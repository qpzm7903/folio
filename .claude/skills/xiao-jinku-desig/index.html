小金库 Design System

a quiet folio · est. 2026
小金库 是一个收藏 金句 的小工具。把那些值得记住的句子放进来，让它们在你不经意的时刻——主屏、屏保、桌面——再一次出现。 The system carries two parallel themes: Tea Paper for daytime and Forest Night for evenings. They share spacing, type, and component vocabulary.
